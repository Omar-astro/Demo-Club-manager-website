Lab Overview & Objectives (FR-06)

The Autonomous Rover Workshop provides students with direct exposure to embedded robotics workflows. Participants will interface with 2D LiDAR scanners, configure ROS 2 nodes, tune navigation costmaps, and deploy point-cloud SLAM algorithms directly onto hardware rovers in the Nano Tech testing hall.

Target Audience Undergraduate & Graduate Computer Science, AI, and Nanotechnology Engineering students.
Certification Credit Eligible for CSAI 203 Lab Practical Endorsement & UST Club Activity Record.
schedule

Hands-On Lab Timeline

14:00 - 14:45
ROS 2 Humble Architecture & Telemetry Pipelines Spinning up publishers, subscribers, custom message definitions, and QoS profiles on Jetson testbeds.
14:45 - 15:45
RPLiDAR A1 Sensor Calibration & 2D SLAM Mapping Capturing LaserScan data, running slam_toolbox in synchronous mode, and generating indoor floorplan maps.
15:45 - 16:30
Nav2 Costmaps & Path Planning Optimization Tuning inflation layers, recovery behaviors, and DWB controller parameters for collision-free trajectories.
16:30 - 17:00
Hardware Rover Obstacle Navigation Challenge Live deployment across the Helmy Hall atrium hazard course with autonomous goal waypoint tracking.
