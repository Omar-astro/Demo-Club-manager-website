About Our Society

The Zewail Astronomy Society (ZAS) unites aspiring astrophysicists, observational engineers, and space enthusiasts across all university majors. Rooted in Dr. Ahmed Zewail's visionary scientific ethos, our members monitor deep-sky phenomena, track exoplanetary transits, process raw astro-imaging datasets, and host campus observation sessions open to the entire academic community.

Welcome to Fall 2026 Astronomy Observations & Stargazing Schedule

Welcome all new and returning students to the Fall 2026 academic term! Our scheduled telescope stargazing sessions will be held every other Tuesday on the Science Building Rooftop Observatory Deck starting at 7:30 PM. Weather forecasts, atmospheric seeing conditions, and celestial targets (including Jupiter and Saturn's ring system) will be dispatched here prior to each observation night. All sessions are open to the entire student body—no optical astronomy experience required.
