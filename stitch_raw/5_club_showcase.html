About Our Society

Department of Physics Affiliated

The Zewail Astronomy Society (ZAS) unites aspiring astrophysicists, observational engineers, and space enthusiasts across all university majors. Rooted in Dr. Ahmed Zewail's visionary scientific ethos, our members monitor deep-sky phenomena, track exoplanetary transits, process raw astro-imaging datasets, and host campus observation sessions open to the entire academic community.

wb_twilight Night Sky Surveys Systematic lunar and planetary tracking using high-grade optical Schmidt-Cassegrains.
insights Spectrometry & Code Python-driven astrophotography analysis, transit photometries, and computational modeling.
public Desert Expeditions Termly excursions to Wadi El Rayan and the White Desert for bortle-class-1 sky observations.
